01How it works

One public booking page, one 30-minute consultation, and every consultant's calendar behind it

Client picks a timeFrom the Edusport Consultation page. They can choose a consultant, or leave it on Anyone.
Bookings checks youIt only offers times that fall inside your working hours and aren't blocked in your Outlook calendar.
Teams link, sent for youThe client and the consultant both get a confirmation email and a calendar invite with a Teams link.
The rules30 minutes, weekdays 9:00 to 17:00, at least 24 hours' notice, up to a year ahead.
The booking link

bookings.cloud.microsoft/book/EdusportConsultation@VB001437.onmicrosoft.com

This is the link that goes into the Freshsales email so clients can book themselves in.

02Set up your availability

Do this once. Each consultant sets their own.

  1. 1
    Open BookingsGo to bookings.cloud.microsoft/bookings, sign in with your Edusport Microsoft account and open Edusport Consultation.
  2. 2
    Go to Staff and open your profileClick Staff on the left, select your name, then Edit staff.
  3. 3
    Keep "Events on Office calendar affect availability" tickedThis is what stops a client booking you while you're in a meeting, on a call or on leave. Leave it on.
  4. 4
    Set your working hoursIf you work the standard 9:00 to 17:00, leave Use business hours on. If your days or hours are different, untick it and set your own days and times.
  5. 5
    SaveThen open the booking link above and check your name shows the times you expect.

03Keep your calendar honest

Bookings reads your Outlook calendar. What's in it decides what clients can book.

In your calendarMeetings, calls, personal appointmentsAnything marked Busy, Tentative or Out of office blocks that time on the booking page.
What to doPut everything in Outlook, including the dentist and the school run. If it isn't there, a client can book over it.
In your calendarEvents set to FreeA Free event does not block anything. Clients can book over it.
What to doOnly use Free for reminders you're happy to be booked over.
Coming upLeave, travel or a day out of the officeYou won't be taking calls, but your working hours still say you're available.
What to doBlock it in Outlook as Out of office, or in Bookings use Calendar, Add time off.

04When a client books

What arrives, and what's still on you

  1. 1
    You get the bookingAn email confirmation and an Outlook invite with the Teams link. The client gets the same. Every booking gets its own Teams meeting, so the link is different each time; you don't have a fixed link of your own.
  2. 2
    PrepareLook the client up in Freshsales before the call. If they used notes on the booking, read them. Come in knowing what they've asked us about.
  3. 3
    Join on timeUse the Teams link in the invite. Clients booked a set slot; treat it like any other client meeting.
  4. 4
    Log it in FreshsalesBookings doesn't create or update anything in Freshsales. Add your notes and next step to the enquiry as you would after any call.
Can't make it?

Open the booking in the Bookings Calendar and reschedule or cancel it there, as early as you can. The client is emailed automatically when a booking is updated or cancelled. Then follow up personally; don't let the system email be the only contact.

When a client replies to their booking email

Replies don't go to the consultant. They go to enquiries@edusport.co.za, which already adds the email as a note on the client's Freshsales record if we know them. Whoever is watching enquiries@ handles them like this:

The replyReschedule, cancel or a question about the callUsually time sensitive, often close to the call itself.
What to doPass it to the consultant on the booking straight away (check the Bookings Calendar if you're not sure who). If they're out, move or cancel it in the Calendar yourself.
The replyFrom someone we don't have in FreshsalesNothing is added to Freshsales automatically.
What to doForward it to new.enquiries@edusport.co.za, as with any new contact, and let the consultant on the booking know.

05How it works with Freshsales

The invitation goes out in a Freshsales email; the booking and the Teams call happen in Microsoft

  1. 1
    The client gets the linkThe client gets a Freshsales email with a Book your call button linking to the booking page. It's one link for every consultant, so nobody needs their own version. Exactly how and when that email goes out is still to be agreed.
  2. 2
    The client booksThey open the booking page, pick a time and, if they want to, a consultant. Bookings only offers times when a consultant is free in Outlook.
  3. 3
    Microsoft sets up the callBookings creates a Teams meeting for that booking and emails the client and the consultant. Every booking gets its own Teams link.
  4. 4
    Back in FreshsalesNothing is updated automatically. The consultant logs the call, notes and next step on the enquiry, as after any call.

08Quick answers

A client says there are no times showing for me. Why?

One of four things: you're not assigned to the service; your Outlook calendar is busy then; it's outside your working hours in Bookings; or it's less than 24 hours away. Check them in that order.

A client wants to cancel on the day.

They can't do it online within 24 hours, so they'll contact us. Cancel or move the booking yourself in the Bookings Calendar.

Can a client pick who they speak to?

Yes. Choosing a consultant is optional on the booking page; if they leave it on Anyone, Bookings assigns someone who's free.

Do I have my own Teams link for bookings?

No. Bookings creates a new Teams meeting for every booking and sends the link to you and the client. Only client replies to booking emails go to enquiries@; the booking itself and the Teams invite come straight to you, as long as you're assigned to the service.

Does a booking show up in Freshsales?

No, not yet. Log the call and your notes on the client's enquiry yourself.

I'm going on leave. What do I do?

Put it in Outlook as Out of office, or use Add time off in the Bookings Calendar. Either one blocks your slots.
